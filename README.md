# 🇬🇧 My English Hub

A simple and modern English learning hub designed to help learners improve their vocabulary and understand essential English concepts through practical, organized resources.

🌐 **Live Website:**  
[My English Hub](https://isaac-maker.github.io/My-EnglishHub/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## ✨ Features

- 📖 **English Verb Dictionary** — Explore English verbs, their meanings, and different verb forms.
- 🧩 **Phrasal Verbs** — Learn common separable and inseparable phrasal verbs.
- 🍷 **Urban Dining** — Useful English vocabulary related to restaurants and dining.
- 🏋️ **Fitness & Mental Health** — Practical vocabulary for fitness and well-being.
- ⛽ **Gas Station vs Charging Station** — Learn vocabulary related to fuel and electric vehicle charging.
- 🏙️ **The Smart City** — English–Spanish vocabulary related to modern cities and technology.
- 🏠 **Home Office** — Useful vocabulary for working from home.
- 💻 **Remote Work & Coworking** — English–Spanish vocabulary for remote work and coworking environments.

## 🎯 Goal

My English Hub aims to provide a practical collection of English learning resources in one place, making it easier to learn new vocabulary and review useful expressions by topic.
